## Why this license rather than "open source"

The intent is that the work stays freely usable by people who want to use it,
and does not get repackaged and sold by someone else. PolyForm Noncommercial is
a real, lawyer-drafted license written for exactly that, rather than a homemade
notice whose terms nobody can be sure of.

Note that "noncommercial" means this is *source-available*, not OSI-approved
open source, since the OSI definition requires permitting commercial use.
